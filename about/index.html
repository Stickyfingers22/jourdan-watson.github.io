The Story

Seoul,
then everywhere

I spent four years of my childhood, from age four to eight, living in South Korea. Growing up between two cultures early made new places feel less like a disruption and more like a puzzle to figure out.

That curiosity stuck. I've now visited 16 places, and I tend to come back from each one with more questions than answers about how people live, work and spend their money, which is part of what pulled me toward economics and data in the first place.
